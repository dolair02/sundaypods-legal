Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pair with my partner?
Open Pulse on both devices, tap the QR / Pair Code button, and have one partner enter the 6-digit code displayed on the other device.

Why didn't my partner feel the vibration?
Ensure your partner has Haptics enabled in their Apple Watch Settings (Sounds & Haptics > Haptic Alerts turned ON). For the strongest feel, select 'Prominent' haptics.

How do I use the Apple Watch Ultra Action Button?
Go to Apple Watch Settings > Action Button > App > Select Pulse. Now pressing the orange button will instantly send your heartbeat pulse.
