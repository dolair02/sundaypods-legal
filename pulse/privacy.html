Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Your privacy is deeply sacred to us. Pulse: Haptic Touch & Love Tap ("we", "our", or "the App") is engineered with an intimate, privacy-first architecture.

1. Zero Account Registration

Pulse does not require an account, email address, phone number, or password. Devices pair directly using short-lived anonymous cryptographic pair codes.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, harvest, or monetize your personal touch data or intimate communications:

  • Haptic Signals: Vibration patterns and tap timestamps are transmitted ephemerally to your paired partner's device and are not stored permanently on any central servers.
  • Battery Status: Battery level information is shared solely with your explicitly linked partner device.
  • Diagnostics & Analytics: Aggregate, anonymized crash reports may be provided via Apple StoreKit and TestFlight under Apple's standard terms.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks. Ever.
