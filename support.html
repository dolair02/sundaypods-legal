Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Thursday matching algorithm work?
Every Thursday at 6:00 PM, our energy-matching algorithm curates balanced 4-person tables. Rather than swiping photos, we balance conversational rhythms (e.g. 2 ambiverts, 1 introvert, 1 extrovert) with complementary humor and shared neighborhood proximity to ensure lively, relaxed discussions with zero awkward silence.
How do I find my table at the cafe?
Each matched pod is assigned a reserved table with a physical mascot (such as The Brass Forest Frog on Table #4), outfit color clues from fellow podders, and a secret handshake phrase ("Catan or Cascadia?"). You can also activate the in-app Table Beacon radar.
How do I redeem my Weekly Cafe Perk Pass?
When your pod meets on Sunday morning, open your active pod screen and tap "Perk Pass". Present the dynamic voucher QR code to the barista at the checkout counter for your $5–$8 complimentary handcrafted coffee or pastry.
How do I manage or cancel my Sunday Premium subscription?
All memberships include a 3-Day Free Trial ($0). If you wish to cancel, you can do so anytime in your iPhone or iPad Settings:
1. Open the Settings app on your device.
2. Tap your Apple ID / Name at the top.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select Sunday Pods and tap Cancel Subscription.
How do I report a safety concern or member violation?
Sunday Pods maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, or aggressive behavior. You can submit an anonymous flag during the post-event Vibe Check or email our trust & safety team directly at dolair02@gmail.com.
