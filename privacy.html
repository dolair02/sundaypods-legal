Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026 · Effective Date: September 30, 2026

At Sunday Pods ("we", "our", or "us"), your privacy and trust are our highest priorities. Sunday Pods was intentionally designed as an antidote to swiping-based dating and noisy social media. We believe real-world human friendship flourishes when your personal data remains private and protected.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to match you into balanced weekend coffee pods and provide our core service:

  • Account Details: Name, email address, and optional avatar image.
  • Social & Energy Preferences: Your self-reported introversion/extroversion calibration, conversational style preferences, and general interests used strictly to calibrate 4-person table compatibility.
  • Neighborhood Selection: Your general neighborhood or borough (e.g., Williamsburg, Brooklyn) to match you with nearby local cafes. We do NOT track precise real-time GPS locations in the background.
  • Table Memories & Reflections: Optional photos you choose to capture for your pod scrapbooks, or voice snippets you choose to contribute to Sunday Morning Radio.

2. What We NEVER Do

  • We NEVER sell your data: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or contact details to third-party brokers or advertisers.
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers: Sunday Pods contains zero third-party behavioral advertising networks or tracking pixels.
  • No Continuous Background Tracking: We do not monitor your physical movements throughout the week.

3. Ephemeral Communication

Pod group chats are strictly temporary (48-hour lifecycle). Once the weekend event concludes, chat histories expire and are permanently purged to prevent data clutter and preserve member privacy.

4. Device Permissions

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used only when you intentionally snap a table polaroid or upload a scrapbook photo.
  • Microphone: Used only when you intentionally record a 20-second voice reflection for Sunday Morning Radio. Audio is never recorded passively.

5. In-App Purchases & Apple ID Billing

All subscriptions and transactions are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit 2. Sunday Pods does not receive, store, or process your credit card or financial billing information.

6. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You have the right to request access to, export, or permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time. You can delete your account directly inside the app settings or by emailing our privacy officer at dolair02@gmail.com. Account deletions are processed within 24 hours.
